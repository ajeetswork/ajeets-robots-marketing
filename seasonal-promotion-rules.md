# Seasonal Promotion Rules

> For the Ajeet's Robots marketing team
>
> These rules govern how to apply seasonal classifications when planning campaigns, ad spend, email sends, blog features, and social media promotion.
>
> **Source of truth for product seasonality:** [Ajeet's Robots Seasonal Promotion Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1d3ooC06vOOubBbVteHSSaknAaq5PaXSea5jBHH_RAEk/edit)

## 2. General promotion rules

### 2.1 In-season: promote freely

When a product is in its best promotion window, it is cleared for:
- Paid ad spend (all channels)
- Email campaign features
- Blog post features
- Homepage / collection page featuring
- Social media promotion
- Group order / bulk consultation recommendations

No additional approval is required beyond normal campaign review processes.

### 2.2 Off-season: proceed with caution

When a product is outside its best promotion window, do **NOT** feature it as a primary campaign product without documenting why.

If you want to promote an off-season product, include in your campaign brief:
1. **Why this product, why now?** — What is the specific reason for featuring it out of season?
2. **Expected performance impact** — Acknowledge that conversion rates and ROAS are likely to be lower than in-season
3. **Budget cap** — Limit off-season test spend (suggested: ≤ 15% of campaign budget for off-season products)
4. **Kill criteria** — At what performance threshold do you pause? Define this before launch, not after

**This is a speed bump, not a ban.** There are legitimate reasons to promote off-season products — see §3 Exceptions below. The rule exists to prevent *accidental* off-season promotion (e.g., featuring a wool hoodie in a July email because someone grabbed the wrong product image).

### 2.3 Never promote unpublished products

Four products in the catalog are currently **unpublished** (`visible = false` in Printify):
- Ajeet Robot mens tee – Apparel – $23.02–$30.22
- Ajeets Robots - Classic Notebook – Home & Office – $23.95
- Ajeets Robots - Classic Water Bottle – Drinkware – $44.42
- Ajeets Robots - Phone Case – Tech – $32.00

**Do not feature unpublished products in any customer-facing campaign.** Ever. No exceptions.

If you want to publish one of these products, follow the [Printify Publication Change Approval Process](https://github.com/ajeetswork/ajeets-robots-operations/blob/main/printify-publication-change-approval.md) — do NOT flip the `visible` flag without a written approval record.

## 3. Exceptions

Seasonal tags are guidance, not hard blocks. The following exceptions allow off-season promotion when justified.

### 3.1 Gift items

**Rule:** Any product — regardless of seasonal tag — may be promoted during gift-giving periods if it works as a gift.

**Gift windows to consider:**
- **Holiday season:** Nov – Dec (broad gift buying)
- **Valentine's Day:** Early Feb
- **Mother's Day / Father's Day:** May – Jun
- **Graduation season:** May – Jun
- **Back-to-school gifting:** Jul – Aug (dorm room, teacher gifts)

**What makes a good gift product:**
- Universal sizing / one-size items (mugs, tote bags, phone stands, wall art, journal, coasters, pet accessories)
- Products at common gift price points ($20–$50 sweet spot, some up to $75)
- Products with broad audience appeal (not size/fit dependent)

**Gift-exception products in the current catalog:**
| Product | Seasonal tag | Why it works as a gift |
|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| AJEETS ROBOTS - Classic Mug | Cold Weather | Classic gift item, $20.99, one size, universally useful |
| Ajeets Robots - Canvas Wall Art Print | Year-Round | Home decor gift, wide price range ($21–$253) covers multiple gift budgets |
| journal | Back-to-School | Great stocking stuffer / New Year gift, $24.99 |
| Ajeets Robots - Phone Stand | Year-Round | Universal fit, all smartphones, $23.48 — easy gift, no sizing risk |
| Ajeets Robots - Wireless Earbuds | Year-Round | Tech gift, $41.99 |
| Pet Bowl / Pet Tag / Pet Bed | Year-Round | Pet owner gifts — strong holiday category |
| Cork Coaster Set | Year-Round | $24.99, home gift, no sizing |

**Gift exception requirements:**
- Marketing copy must frame the product as a gift ("perfect for …", "gift guide", etc.) — do not pretend it's in-season for its primary use case
- Do NOT claim a product is "the perfect summer gift" if its seasonal tag is Cold Weather — frame honestly: "cozy gift for the colder months ahead" is fine in November; "summer essential" is not
- Gift promotion does not override the unpublished product ban (§2.3)

### 3.2 Indoor / climate-controlled products

**Rule:** Products used primarily indoors, in climate-controlled environments, are less sensitive to seasonal swings. Their seasonal tag still applies for primary campaign planning, but the off-season penalty is lower.

**Indoor products in the current catalog:**

| journal | Back-to-School | Journaling/note-taking happens year-round indoors. Back-to-school is the peak, but not exclusive. |
| Ajeets Robots - Classic Mousepad | Back-to-School | Desk accessory — used year-round in offices/home offices. Back-to-school is peak refresh timing. |
| Ajeets Robots - Classic Notebook | Back-to-School | Same as journal — indoor use, year-round utility, seasonal peak at back-to-school. (UNPUBLISHED) |
| Ajeets Robots - Canvas Wall Art Print | Year-Round | Already Year-Round tagged — included here for completeness |
| Ajeets Robots - Square Pillowcase | Year-Round | Home decor — indoor, climate-controlled, year-round |
| Ajeets Robots - Classic Shower Curtain | Year-Round | Bathroom — indoor, year-round |
| Cork Coaster Set | Year-Round | Home/kitchen — indoor, year-round |

**Indoor exception — what changes:**
- Off-season ROAS penalty is typically 20–40% (vs 50–80% for outdoor/seasonal apparel)
- These products can fill campaign slots during slow periods without major performance risk
- Still respect the primary seasonal tag for *headliner* campaign placement — use indoor products as supporting/filler items off-season, not campaign anchors

**Indoor exception — what does NOT change:**
- A Cold Weather tagged mug is still Cold Weather tagged. Don't market it as a summer iced coffee tumbler if the product description is for hot beverages.
- Do not invent use cases that aren't in the product listing. If the Printify product description says "morning coffee" and "slow sips", don't reframe it as a "summer iced latte mug" — that's not what the listing says.
- All claims must match the product listing. See the [Accessibility Information Review Framework](https://github.com/ajeetswork/ajeets-robots-product-research/blob/main/accessibility-information-review-framework.md) §4 — describe physical attributes factually, don't invent use cases.

## 4. Campaign planning checklist

Before locking a campaign, run through this checklist:

- [ ] Every featured product has a seasonal tag in the [Seasonal Promotion Map](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1d3ooC06vOOubBbVteHSSaknAaq5PaXSea5jBHH_RAEk/edit)
- [ ] Every featured product is **published** (`visible = true` in Printify) — check, don't assume
- [ ] For each product, is the campaign month within its Best Promotion Months?
  - If **yes** → proceed
  - If **no** → is there a valid exception? (Gift item §3.1 / Indoor product §3.2 / Year-Round staple §3.3)
    - If **yes, with exception** → document the exception reason in the campaign brief
    - If **no exception applies** → swap the product for an in-season alternative, OR document off-season justification per §2.2 (reason, expected performance impact, budget cap, kill criteria)
- [ ] No product listing claims contradict the seasonal framing (e.g., don't run a "summer essentials" campaign featuring a product whose description says "cozy fleece for cold winter mornings")
- [ ] Pricing in campaign creative matches current Printify retail price — **do NOT advertise discount prices unless a formal price change has gone through the Publication Change Approval Process**
- [ ] For group order / bulk consultation recommendations: warn buyers that Printify is print-on-demand with **no built-in bulk discount / MOQ**, lead times vary by print provider, confirm fulfillment timeline before committing to event dates

## 6. What NOT to do

- ❌ **Do not change Printify retail prices for seasonal promotions** — pricing changes require a [Publication Change Approval](https://github.com/ajeetswork/ajeets-robots-operations/blob/main/printify-publication-change-approval.md) with 2 approvers, 24h minimum lead time, and a rollback plan. If you want to run a sale/discount campaign, get pricing approval first — do not advertise prices that don't match the live Printify listing.
- ❌ **Do not invent seasonal use cases** — if a mug's product description is about "morning coffee" and "slow sips", don't market it as a "summer iced tea essential". Match your campaign framing to what's actually in the product listing.
- ❌ **Do not feature unpublished products** — 4 products in the catalog are currently `visible = false`. They do not appear on the storefront. Do not include them in any customer-facing campaign, even as "coming soon".
- ❌ **Do not ignore the seasonal tag entirely** — the tags exist to prevent accidental off-season promotion. If you have a good reason to promote out of season, document it (§2.2). Don't just ignore the tag.
- ❌ **Do not make medical / accessibility / health claims** — see the [Accessibility Information Review Framework](https://github.com/ajeetswork/ajeets-robots-product-research/blob/main/accessibility-information-review-framework.md) §4. Describe products factually. No ADA claims, no medical device claims, no disability-specific suitability claims.
- ❌ **Do not claim bulk discounts / MOQ pricing** — Printify is print-on-demand with no built-in volume pricing. Always tell group buyers to negotiate volume pricing with the print provider directly.
